Gynecologic Ultrasound

Gynecologic ultrasound allows for a professional assessment of the pelvic organs, including Doppler evaluation of blood flow and 3D imaging of the uterine cavity, for personalized diagnosis.

When to Come for the Exam

The exam is suited for both routine follow-up and focused evaluation, as needed.

01 Routine exam as part of periodic follow-up
02 Evaluating complaints: pain, irregular bleeding or discomfort
03 Monitoring a known finding over time
04 As part of an infertility work-up
02
Frequently Asked Questions
How is the exam performed?

The exam is performed using a combined abdominal-transvaginal approach when possible, according to medical need.

Is special preparation required?

In most cases no special preparation is needed. In certain cases you may be asked to arrive with a full bladder or at a specific point in your menstrual cycle — precise instructions will be given when scheduling the appointment.

How long does the exam take?

A routine exam usually takes about half an hour, depending on findings and the purpose of the exam.

Does the exam carry any risk?

This is a standard ultrasound exam, with no radiation, that carries no risk.

Still have questions?

Feel free to reach out for more information or to schedule an exam.
